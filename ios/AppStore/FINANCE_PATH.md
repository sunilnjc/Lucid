# Finance & Accounting: first complete path

90 words · 30 lessons · 6 chapters · choose 1–3 new words per day.

This is a professional-English curriculum, not accounting training or advice. Each lesson has three target words, an objective, a fictional workplace challenge and a model response. At one word per day, a lesson spans three days; “30 lessons” does not promise completion in 30 calendar days.

| Chapter | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Close the month clearly | Explain open balances, timing, approvals and handovers |
| Explain what changed | Separate drivers, comparisons, significance and balanced commentary |
| Discuss the future with care | Explain assumptions, scenarios, cash timing and revision triggers |
| Make the work defensible | Connect evidence, checks, responsibilities and open issues |
| Help people decide | Clarify, recommend, challenge constructively and agree ownership |
| Bring the whole picture together | Produce a concise executive update and follow-up plan |

## Try it locally

1. Build/run the Lucid scheme in Xcode using Debug on your simulator or paired iPhone.
2. On Welcome, choose Personalise my Lucid. Select Finance & Accounting, at least one situation and goal, then Build my first lesson. Existing learners can change their role in Settings without erasing history.
3. Today → Your professional path opens the roadmap. Progress also links to it.
4. Open a lesson, inspect its word cards, write a fictional practice response and compare with the example. The draft persists on this device.
5. Use Today to practise the selected words. A preview alone never counts as learning; spaced-review mastery is separate from “words started”. Existing same-day plans survive upgrades, so a returning learner may first finish the plan already in progress.

## Content and scope

The canonical source is `lib/professional-content.ts`; `npm run ios:content` exports the catalogue and paths to the native resource. All 11 original Finance-active entries are preserved, with 79 additional Finance entries. All eight roles now have equivalent 90-word, 30-lesson paths and an optional starting-point check. See [PROFESSIONAL_COURSES.md](PROFESSIONAL_COURSES.md).

The independent editorial pass corrected nine fields across six lessons. A real Finance professional and a small learner pilot should still assess language usefulness, challenge clarity, difficulty and actual workplace transfer before public launch. Pattern matching is not an assessment of grammar or meaning.
